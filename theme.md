# Bobert the Adventurer: Theme

Everything I've asked for in the game, in the order I asked for it.

## Requests so far

- Spawn in a village with stone houses.
- You have a sword and shield.
- A merchant comes to you and says, in exact quotes: "The village as we know it is in imminent danger you must slay a Gelationus Slime to retrieve our villages precious artifact. you will be rewarded handsomely"
- Get rid of the cherry blossom trees.
- Add texture to the trees.
- Get rid of the orange and birch trees.
- Press Return to swing the sword.
- Add a cave with the slime inside.
- The slime takes 10 hits to kill, and it takes 5 attacks to kill you.
- Add villagers who walk around and give you side quests.
- Side quests include finding things around the world, defeating monsters and doing puzzles.
- Add a tunnel through a mountain to the slime. The cave can have chests, and other monsters that take two hits to kill.
- Add a shop where you can buy a better sword. You can also buy a shield there.
- You don't start with a shield.
- Once you buy the shield from the shop, press J to use it. You can't use your sword while shielding.
- When you spawn in and get the artifact, the merchant doesn't come to you.
- Pay 50 coins at the Forge to upgrade it and get other weapons, like a bow, a better shield and armor that increases your max health.
- Add a side quest to go out to sea, to an island with a tower on it, to get a mystery crystal.
- Add more details to the houses.
- Make it easier to look around.
- Spread the trees out.
- Go to the dock to start swimming. You can swim around in the sea.
- Add eels and sharks.
- Sharks have 3 health and do 1 damage.
- Eels have 5 health and do 3 damage.
- Add food that you can buy from a different vendor. It heals you, and you can carry five with you.
- Add a path from the town to the dock.
- The sea is bigger and has small islands.
- There is no boat.
- The mystery crystal unlocks the trapdoor outside of town, which leads to Old Man Keegan. Talk to him to get the quest "Slay Charibdis".
- To get the key, kill either the Kraken or the Sea Serpent.
- The Sea Serpent is a big, fast eel with 10 health.
- The Kraken has 10 health and does 5 damage.
- The key unlocks Charibdis's cave, which is full of water. Charibdis looks like a fish with a huge mouth and is covered in eyes.
- Charibdis has 30 health, and does 5 damage every 3 seconds to anyone in the small whirlpool around it.
- The whirlpool turns off every 5 seconds, for 9 seconds.
- The bow takes one second to fire and starts with 10 arrows. You can buy 10 more arrows at the Forge for 20 coins.
- Once you've fired all your arrows, you can't fire any more until you buy more.
- Change the prices at the Forge.
- The bow can carry 100 arrows at once.
- Make enemies slower.
- Make sword swings slower.
- Make the tower taller and add gargoyles. They're very weak monsters, but there are quite a few of them: not too many, but enough to be a problem.
- Make sword slashes horizontal, so they can hit several enemies at once.
- The game goes through a day and night cycle every two minutes.
- The day and night cycle changes nothing but the lighting, and villagers go to sleep in their houses at night.
- Eels are rarer than sharks.
- Make the side quests a bit harder.
- Make the swimming animation more realistic.
- Make the slime smaller.
- Add texture to the world.
- Lower the prices of apples and bread.
- Over time, villagers give more quests.
- Get rid of the Knight's Sword at the Forge.
- Add an axe and a pickaxe to get wood and stone, for upgrading things at the Forge and the food vendor.
- Upgraded Forge items include the Knight's Sword and the Power Bow. The Power Bow does 1 damage for every 2 seconds you charge it, up to 5 damage.
- Some upgraded foods give you temporary health: extra hearts that go away once you take that much damage.
- Make the island not perfectly circular.
- The town, the merchant and the other people who sell you things go inside for the night.
- You don't get other side quests until you've done the merchant's quest.
- Once you kill Charibdis and talk to Old Man Keegan, Captain Finn takes you to a jungle.
- The jungle has lots of different monsters, like the giant Venus flytrap. It attacks slowly and can't move, but does 5 damage when it bites down on you. It has 1 health.
- Pressing Command lets you change the controls.
- Make Charibdis's lair bigger.
- Make the tower at sea much bigger.
- Get rid of the quest and controls menus. You can open them by pressing Option.
- Don't change the camera angle when you walk under the trees.
- Make the trees taller.
- Add more sharks in the ocean.
- Monsters follow you until you get fairly far away from them.
- Monsters are stronger at night.
- Once you've talked to Captain Finn, you can start swimming from any beach.
- Make the Forge more like the food vendor: you build a bigger Forge with wood and stone to get all the upgraded things.
- Make the island bigger.
- Add more houses and villagers.
- When you swim, you go underwater. This means all the sea monsters are underwater too.
- Add a boat that costs 40 wood, so you can go to the jungle or explore the ocean. It's unlocked once you've been to the jungle.
- Add a boss called the Vine. It has 50 health and arms that slam down on you like the Kraken's, doing 5 damage.
- Add monsters in the jungle, like snakes.
- Add an upgraded pickaxe and axe to the upgraded Forge, to get more wood and stone.
- Once you defeat the Vine, the boss of the jungle, you unlock a dream dimension that gives you special quests.
- Doing the special quests in the dream dimension gives you dream points. With enough dream points, you can unlock abilities like dashing or turning invisible.
- Somewhere in the jungle, add an NPC named Master Oak, who teaches you a spin attack. You do it by holding down the sword button.
- To learn the spin attack, you have to beat Oak in a fight.
- Oak is very strong and does 10 damage if he hits you, but he only has 8 health.
- Add more dream shrine abilities.
- Add a railing to the tower.
- Make it always an overhead view.
- Gargoyles can't fly through walls.
- Increase enemy attack speed by 25%.
